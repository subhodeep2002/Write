Write.

A dark, distraction-free document editor for your Mac. It works like Google Docs, even with no internet, and can sync with your Google Docs when you want it to.

Download for Mac How to install

Free · macOS 12 or newer · Apple Silicon (M1 and later)

What Write. does

Works offlineEverything is saved on your Mac as you type. No account and no internet needed.
Feels like Google DocsThe same menus, toolbar, pages and ruler: headings, fonts, colours, lists, tables, images, comments, find & replace and page setup.
Document tabsSplit a long document into tabs and subtabs, like Google Docs. Download one tab or all of them.
Spelling and grammarInstant checking that runs on your Mac. Nothing you write is sent anywhere to be checked.
Download anythingSave as Word (.docx), PDF, web page, Markdown or plain text.
Version historyEarlier versions are kept automatically, so you can look back and restore one.

Google Docs sync (optional)

  1. In Write., open Settings and click Sign in with Google. Your browser opens; choose your account and allow access.
  2. Click Import from Google Docs. Google's own file picker opens in Google Chrome. Choose the documents you want.
  3. That's it. Imported documents keep all their tabs and stay linked: edits in Write. sync back to Google Docs, and you can pause syncing any time.

Write. can only see the Google Docs you pick or create with it, never the rest of your Drive. It talks to Google directly from your Mac; there are no Write. servers. Read the privacy policy.

Importing needs Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge), because Google's file picker doesn't work in Safari.
