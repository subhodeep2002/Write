Features

Everything you need to write.
Nothing you don't.

Document tabs

Split a long document into tabs and subtabs, like Google Docs. One per chapter, if you like.

Spelling and grammar

Checked instantly as you type, on your Mac. Nothing you write is sent anywhere.

Real pages

Pages, a ruler, margins and page setup for Letter, A4 or Legal. Or go pageless.

Comments

Leave notes and replies next to the text, and resolve them when you're done.

Version history

Earlier versions are kept automatically. Look back at any of them and restore it.

Download anything

Word, PDF, web page, Markdown or plain text. Open Word and Markdown files too.

Right-click menu

Tables

Markdown shortcuts

Google Docs

Your Google Docs,
when you want them.

  1. 1

    Sign in once

    Open Settings and click Sign in with Google. No keys, no setup.

  2. 2

    Pick your documents

    Google's own file picker opens in Google Chrome. Choose one document or several.

  3. 3

    Stay in sync

    They come in with all their tabs. Edits in Write. sync back, or pause syncing whenever you like.

Write. can only see the Google Docs you pick or create with it, never the rest of your Drive. It talks to Google directly from your Mac; there are no Write. servers. Read the privacy policy.
