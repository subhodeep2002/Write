Google Docs

Your Google Docs
whenever you want them

  1. 1

    Sign in once

    Open Settings and click Sign in with Google and that's really all there is to it

  2. 2

    Pick your documents

    Google's own file picker opens in Chrome so you can choose one document or as many as you like

  3. 3

    Stay in sync

    Your documents arrive with all their tabs and every edit you make flows back to Google Docs unless you pause it

Write. can only see the documents you pick or create with it and it talks to Google straight from your Mac with no servers of ours in between. There's more about this in the privacy policy.
